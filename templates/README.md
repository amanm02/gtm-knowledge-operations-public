# Templates

The templates in this directory show how governed GTM Brain records become reusable shared, Marketing, Sales, and CX assets.

They are static Markdown contracts. They are not generated outputs.

## Common contract

Every template includes:

- Asset ID;
- team and asset type;
- status and owner;
- Audience, Source, Evidence, Insight, Claim, and Message references;
- sensitivity and external-use policy;
- created, reviewed, and next-review dates;
- known gaps and restrictions;
- feedback to GTM Brain;
- review decision fields.

Bracketed values are placeholders.

## Catalog

| Shared | Marketing | Sales | CX |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research synthesis | Content brief | Account research brief | Voice-of-customer summary |
| Messaging foundation | Campaign brief | Discovery guide | Onboarding brief |
| Evidence register | Positioning brief | Objection handling | Adoption risk brief |
| Knowledge gap log | Voice-of-customer brief | Competitive battlecard | Renewal and expansion brief |

## Usage boundary

A template does not approve its content.

Before reuse, an owner must:

- resolve record references;
- verify freshness;
- preserve restrictions;
- review known gaps;
- record the decision separately;
- set the next review date.

The public repository contains placeholders only.
